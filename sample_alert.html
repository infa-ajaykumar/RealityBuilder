Sample Alert Page for Testing

Use this page to test your extension's DOM selectors. Open Chrome DevTools (Inspect) to find the correct classes.

For this example page, the selectors would be:

  • ALERT_ROW_SELECTOR: '.alert-list-item'
  • ALERT_TEXT_SELECTOR: '.alert-message'
  • ACK_BUTTON_SELECTOR: 'button.acknowledge-button'
  • CLOSE_BUTTON_SELECTOR: 'button.close-button'
